Noodles

Description

Ingredients

  • 200 g noodles
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1–2 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Steps

Noodles are made by boiling noodles in water until tender and then draining them. In a pan, vegetables such as onions, carrots, bell peppers, and cabbage are stir-fried with a little oil. The cooked noodles are added along with soy sauce, salt, and pepper, then mixed well and cooked for a few more minutes. The noodles are served hot and can be enjoyed on their own or with chicken, eggs, or other protein.
