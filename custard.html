Custard

Description

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp custard powder (vanilla flavor)
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar
  • Mixed fruits (such as apples, bananas, grapes, and mangoes), chopped

Steps

Custard is made by mixing custard powder with a little cold milk to form a smooth paste. The remaining milk is heated with sugar until warm, then the custard mixture is added and stirred continuously until it thickens. After cooling, chopped fruits are mixed into the custard. It is then chilled in the refrigerator and served cold as a dessert.
