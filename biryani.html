Biryani

Description

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • 500 g chicken (bone-in or boneless)
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Juice of ½ lemon

for the rice

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 6–8 cups water
  • 2–3 green cardamoms
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2–3 cloves
  • salt

Steps

Chicken biryani is made by marinating chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, and salt, then cooking it with fried onions until tender. Separately, basmati rice is boiled until about three-quarters cooked. The chicken and rice are then layered in a pot with fried onions, mint, and coriander leaves. The pot is covered tightly and cooked on low heat for about 20–25 minutes so the flavors blend together. The biryani is then gently mixed and served hot, often with raita or salad.
